## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an ethos of **Sophisticated Eco-Luxury & Dignified Modernism**. Built for a high-end smart farm community village, it reconciles agrarian serenity with vanguard agrotech innovation. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Serene & Architectural:** The aesthetic projects stability, high taste, and organic permanence. It avoids rustic tropes in favor of editorial, botanical minimalism.
- **Trustworthy & Visionary:** Smart farming, precision IoT, and community investment require an atmosphere of sovereign quality and transparent stewardship.
- **Target Audience:** Discerning retirees, second-life innovators, wellness investors, and eco-conscious urbanites seeking sustainable, high-tech pastoral life.

### Design Movement: Eco-Editorial Glassmorphism
The visual direction fuses pristine editorial layouts (refined serif display headers, generous vertical breathing room, generous tracking) with high-end digital craftsmanship:
- Translucent frosted surfaces reminiscent of high-tech greenhouse glass panes.
- Luminous ambient depth tinted with forest undertones.
- Polished brass/champagne gold micro-accents providing warmth and architectural refinement.

## Colors

The palette establishes a balance between rich botanical depth and soft architectural warmth. It shifts away from stark monoculture green into stratified mineral and flora tones.

### Color Tiers & Roles
- **Primary (`#0F4A36`, `#165B43`):** Deep Forest Emerald. Serves as the bedrock of visual trust, grounding key narrative blocks, primary action surfaces, and dominant branding.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#D4AF37`, `#C29B38`, Light: `#F4E8C1`):** Warm Champagne Gold. Used selectively for bespoke badges, precision borders, highlights, and prestige accolades.
- **Tertiary / Vitality Point (`#2ECC71`, `#25A25A`):** Young Leaf Green. Represents smart-farm sensor telemetry, living crops, fresh yield metrics, and growth indicators.
- **Neutral Light & Surfaces (`#F6F9F7`, `#EBF3EE`, `#FFFFFF`):** Soft Pale Sage and Warm Ivory. Forms the luminous background canvas, replacing sterile grey with an organic tint.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1A231F`, `#2C3E35`):** Deep Slate Ebony. Used for ultra-legible editorial body copy, captions, and structural divider lines.

### Functional Roles
- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F6F9F7`
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Module Canvas):** `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Level 2 (Frosted Inset Canvas):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` over `#EBF3EE`
- **Surface Level 3 (Deep Inverted Island):** `#0F4A36`
- **Text Primary:** `#1A231F`
- **Text Muted / Botanical Charcoal:** `#42544B`
- **Text Inverted:** `#F6F9F7`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary dignity of classical Korean/Latin serif forms with the ergonomic functionality of a clean, structured neo-grotesque sans-serif.

### Type Pairings & Behavioral Intent
- **Display & Section Headers (`Noto Serif`):** Conveys prestige, stewardship, and literary weight. Used for hero headings, value proposition catchphrases, and key conceptual anchors. Generous line-height (`1.3`–`1.4`) prevents dense cluster traps, while slightly tightened tracking creates editorial poise.
- **Structural UI, Metrics, & Body Text (`Noto Sans`):** Ensures clear scanning on mobile viewports. Korean body copy mandates a relaxed `line-height` (`1.6`–`1.7`) to give complex Hangul glyphs ample internal breathing room.
- **Prestige Eyebrows & Data Badges (`label-md`, `label-sm`):** Set in all-caps (Latin) or tracked sans-serif (Hangul) with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em`–`0.12em`) to mirror luxury architectural signage.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an editorial fluid grid with a strictly enforced 8pt spatial cadence. It creates an uncluttered, quiet rhythm that emphasizes organic tranquility.

### Mobile-First Layout Model
- **Mobile Grid (Base):** Single-column fluid viewport with a mandatory inline page margin of `20px` (`gutter-mobile`). Visual containers and glassmorphic cards extend to the safe bounds without suffocating content.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major thematic transitions utilize `section-gap-mobile` (`64px`) to preserve psychological pacing and prevent dense commercial clutter.
- **Tablet Reflow (600px - 1023px):** Shifts to a 6-column fluid grid, `24px` gutters, grouping smart farm facility specs and community lifestyle perks into alternating split cards.
- **Desktop Canvas (1024px+):** Centers on a maximum content wrapper of `1200px` within a 12-column architecture, flanked by soft sage-tinted margins.

### Elevation Levels

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses generous curved radiuses (`rounded-2xl` for cards, `rounded-full` for interactive pills). This softens the visual impact, reflecting natural organic curves rather than rigid industrial geometry.

### Radius Assignments
- **Primary Cards & Modals:** `1.5rem` (`24px`). Creates continuous, curved enclosures that balance sharp typography.
- **Nested Insets & Micro Containers:** `1rem` (`16px`). Ensures optical harmony when nested inside Level 1/2 cards.
- **Buttons, Status Badges & Chips:** `62.5rem` (`9999px` / Full Pill). Provides a tactile, polished feel for actions and tags.
- **Form Controls:** `0.875rem` (`14px`). Balances thumb usability with structured stability.
