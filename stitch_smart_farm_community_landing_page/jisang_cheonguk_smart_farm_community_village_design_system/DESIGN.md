## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a dignified, visionary rural regeneration blueprint tailored for institutional stakeholders, foundation directors, and agrarian technologists. The aesthetic merges agricultural heritage with progressive smart-green technology.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Dignified & Reassuring:** Projects financial integrity, institutional viability, and long-term land stewardship without commercial loud-budget flashiness.
- **Eco-Harmonic & Modern:** Integrates advanced agro-tech efficiency with Yangpyeong’s natural topography and ecological mindfulness.
- **Serene & Architectural:** Uses generous whitespace, understated warmth, and editorial restraint to mirror the spacious cadence of agrarian life.

### Design Movement
**Modern Organic Editorial Minimalism:** Clean rectilinear architecture supported by warm, tactile paper-like backgrounds, restrained structural borders, elevated botanical accents, and precise institutional typography.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, dependable hierarchy that honors natural land stewardship while remaining executive and readable.

### Functional Palette Roles
- **Canvas (`#F2F7F4`):** Soft Mint mist applied as the overall system background, reducing eye strain and evoking early morning agrarian dew.
- **Primary Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white applied to cards, data modules, and executive summaries to create clear figure-ground separation.
- **Primary Brand (`#1F6B4F`):** Deep Forest Green anchored to key interactive actions, high-level structural banners, primary navigation markers, and authoritative brand titles.
- **Secondary Accent (`#C9A227`):** Warm Gold reserved for executive milestones, strategic initiatives, yield metrics, certification marks, and refined ornamental highlights.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#3D8D6E`):** Mid-tone Pine Green utilized for telemetry badges, healthy environmental indicators, and subtle active-state fills.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1A202C`):** Deep Obsidian Charcoal for primary headers, quantitative telemetry readouts, and legal/statutory content.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#2D3748`):** Warm Gray for long-form editorial copy, project narratives, and table records.
- **Structural Border (`#E2E8F0`):** Delicate, crisp border tone that bounds containers without harsh visual contrast.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy bridges scholarly permanence and technological lucidity.

- **Headlines & Executive Statements (`Noto Serif`):** Conveys the weight, timelessness, and legal gravitas of a foundation charter. Use for project titles, thesis statements, and architectural section leads.
- **Body & Numerical Analytics (`Noto Sans`):** Selected for uncompromised Korean/Latin legibility across operational telemetry, agronomic sensor reports, balance sheets, and explanatory copy.
- **Leading & Whitespace Ratios:** Body line heights are tuned to 1.7–1.75x to give Korean script comfortable vertical breathing room, avoiding dense ink-trapping in administrative reports.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture is grounded in a 12-column institutional grid that prioritizes breathing space, horizontal alignment, and orderly content grouping.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column layout on desktop (1280px max-width centered, 32px gutters, 48px margins); 8-column layout on tablet (24px gutters, 32px margins); 4-column layout on mobile (16px gutters, 20px margins).
- **Executive Margins:** Macro spacing utilizes `space-3xl` (72px) and `space-4xl` (96px) between thematic village domains (e.g., Agricultural Telemetry vs. Community Endowment vs. Master Plan).
- **Rhythm:** Internal module paddings strictly follow 24px (`space-lg`) or 32px (`space-xl`) to establish a measured, unhurried reading cadence suited to strategic decision-making.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids theatrical drop shadows and hyper-glossy surfaces, adopting an architectural paper-and-canvas physical depth model.

- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F2F7F4` Soft Mint. Serves as the continuous environmental plane.
- **Tier 1 (Structural Modules & Cards):** `#FFFFFF` Pure White bounded by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` hairline boundary. Ambient shadow is virtually absent (`0 1px 3px rgba(31, 107, 79, 0.04)`), creating a grounded, paper-stock presence.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` elevated via a soft diffused botanical shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(31, 107, 79, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 55, 72, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Executive Overlays & Modal Proposals):** `#FFFFFF` surrounded by a 1px accent stroke of `rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.25)` and supported by an ultra-diffused floor shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(26, 32, 44, 0.12)`. Backdrop layer applies an atmospheric blur of `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)` over an 85% opacity tinted canvas.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined shape language that balances corporate sobriety with natural curvature.

- **Containers & Data Boards:** Constructed with soft architectural radii (`0.25rem` / 4px to `0.5rem` / 8px). Corners are disciplined and crisp, framing village master-planning and telemetry with clarity.
- **Badges, Tags, & Status Pills:** Fully rounded capsule/pill contours (`9999px`) deployed for status chips, carbon offset trackers, and phase badges, injecting warmth into the structure.
- **Hairline Precision:** Outer card rims and division rules maintain an absolute 1px stroke weight, reinforcing structural dignity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Executive Action:** Rich Deep Forest Green (`#1F6B4F`) surface with pure white typography, 0.25rem (4px) rounded corners, and generous padding (`12px 28px`). On hover, transitions cleanly to `#18563F` with an understated forward micro-transition.
- **Secondary / Endowment Action:** Crisp pure white container bordered by a 1.5px stroke of Warm Gold (`#C9A227`), housing `#C9A227` text. Designed for charter downloads, financial reports, and secondary inquiries.
- **Tertiary / Link Action:** Clean typography in `#1F6B4F` with an underlined hairline that animates from 0% to 100% width on hover.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Status Indicators:** Pill-shaped capsules (`rounded-full`) with a height of 24px to 28px and horizontal padding of 12px. 
- **Variants:**
  - *Active Facility / Healthy Sensor:* `#F2F7F4` background, `#1F6B4F` label, `#3D8D6E` miniature 6px indicator dot.
  - *Strategic Milestone:* Pale gold tint (`rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.12)`) background, `#967818` label, solid `#C9A227` rim.
  - *Institutional Governance:* `#EDF2F7` neutral background, `#2D3748` label.

### Cards & Narrative Panels
- **Anatomy:** `#FFFFFF` background, a razor-thin 1px border of `#E2E8F0`, and ample internal padding (32px).
- **Header Structure:** Includes an editorial eyebrow tag in `label-sm` uppercase, followed by a `headline-md` title set in Noto Serif, separated from body metrics by an understated hairline divider (`#EDF2F7`).

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Pure white backdrop enclosed within a 1px `#CBD5E1` border.
- **State Feedback:** Focus states remove standard blue rings, shifting to a 1.5px border of `#1F6B4F` complemented by an ethereal focus ring of `rgba(31, 107, 79, 0.12)`.
- **Labels & Hints:** Labels sit above the field in `label-md` Charcoal (`#2D3748`).

### Lists & Telemetry Registers
- **Data Architecture:** Board-ready data tables and metric logs feature alternating rows of `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAF9`.
- **Borders:** Thin horizontal dividers (`#E2E8F0`) with 0px vertical borders maintain horizontal scan efficiency across yield logs, climate parameters, and village ledger updates.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Form Controls:** 18px square (checkbox) or circular (radio) toggles bounded by a 1.5px border in `#A0AEC0`. When active, fills with `#1F6B4F` showcasing a crisp white geometric check or inner disc.

### Foundation-Specific Additions
- **Executive Metric Block:** High-impact statistical module featuring numerical counters in Noto Serif (36px, `#1A202C`), accompanied by small Warm Gold (`#C9A227`) trend indicators and a muted Korean descriptor.
- **Land Stewardship Banner:** Full-width accent frame with a fine `#C9A227` top edge border, pairing high-resolution architectural photography with Deep Forest Green tinted overlays.
